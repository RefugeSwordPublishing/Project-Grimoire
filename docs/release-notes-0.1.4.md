# Project Grimoire, Version 0.1.4 Release Notes

Everything new since 0.1.3d. Highlights first, then fixes and polish.

## Combat

- **New weapons.** The arsenal now spans three bow archetypes (shortbow, longbow, crossbow), a two-handed greataxe, and off-hand shields.
- **Shields and handedness.** One-handed weapons (sword, dagger, axe, wand, crossbow) can now carry a shield for a block chance and added defense. Two-handed weapons (staff, shortbow, longbow, greataxe) trade the off-hand for more raw power.
- **Weapons feel different.** Attack speed now varies by weapon type: lighter weapons swing more often, heavier ones hit harder per swing. Combat runs on a tighter, more responsive tick.
- **Co-op ally cards.** In party combat you now see live cards for your teammates: current HP, active status effects, and downed or left-the-fight states. Tap a card to inspect that partner's gear and stats.
- **Depth in the field.** Combat backgrounds have a subtle parallax sway for a more atmospheric scene.

## Idle and Progression

- **Do two things at once.** Gathering, crafting, and cooking now keep progressing while you are in combat. Set ore to smelt, run a dungeon, and come back to craft the bars, both tracks advance at full rate.
- **Level-up celebration.** On the While You Were Away screen, the levels you gained now rise as celebratory floating toasts, the same way they do during active play.

## Economy and Inventory

- **Sell to the Traveling Merchant.** Sell items one at a time or in bulk for Silver Marks.
- **One quantity control everywhere.** Every "how many?" prompt, selling, eating, sending to a player, and guild bank deposit or withdraw, now works the same way: type a number, drag the slider, or tap Max.

## Social

- **Friends from the chat dock.** The Friends menu is now reachable directly from the chat dock (previously you could only get to it through chat).

## New Art

- New gear icons for the shortbow, crossbow, greataxe, and shield.

## Fixes and Polish

- Fixed a navigation issue that could leave every menu button unresponsive.
- Removed duplicate combat-hub panels and hardened the tools that build them so it cannot recur.
- Zone detail art now fills its banner cleanly instead of stretching.
- Combat-hub XP bar fills correctly with skinned art.
- Auto-eat reworked to be potion-gated (it eats only from the draughts you carry).
- Quest board polish (in-place accordion, a clear "Complete" stamp on finished quests).
- Wayfarer's Exchange "Fresh Market" placement and duplicate-panel fixes.
- Numerous smaller UI and layout fixes across the combat hub, chat dock, and inventory.
